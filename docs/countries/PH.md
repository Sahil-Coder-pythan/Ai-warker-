# PH country launch playbook

Philippines rollout: validate island serviceability, address format, restricted goods, privacy and carrier coverage.

## Launch gate
- Confirm a licensed/contracted carrier and test its sandbox.
- Verify address validation and serviceability using trusted provider data.
- Publish accurate rates, delivery estimates, restrictions, refund and complaint terms.
- Review privacy, consumer protection, tax and customs requirements with qualified local advisors.
- Test local language content with native speakers.
- Do not enable public confirmed booking until the provider adapter and operations are tested.
